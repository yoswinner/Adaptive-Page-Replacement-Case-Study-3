- high_locality : 50 pages; 80% of references to 10 hot pages (pages 0-9),

                  of jumping to a random page
- random        : uniform over 50 pages
- looping       : cyclic 0,1,2,3,4,5 (loop size 6)

5. ADAPTIVE POLICY PARAMETERS (src/adaptive.py defaults, used unchanged)
- Window sizes W           : 50 and 100 (rolling deque of past references)
- Evaluation interval      : when the window is full and step % max(10, W//2) == 0
                             (every 25 refs for W=50, every 50 refs for W=100)
- U < 0.35                 -> LRU
- U >= 0.85                -> Clock
- CV >= 1.0 and U < 0.70   -> LFU
- otherwise                -> LRU (initial strategy: LRU)
